# 30daymapchallenge
funkchan's #30DayMapChallenge maps, data pipelines and styles

## 2026

- **[Day 1 · Points](2026/day01-points/)** — Hackerspaces Across the World: 1,898 hackerspaces in 92 countries mapped from OpenStreetMap
- **[Day 2 · Lines](2026/day02-lines-biomes/)** — The World's Longest Straight Lines, by Biome: geodesics through seven major biomes, grassland winning at 5,806 km
- **[Day 3 · Polygons](2026/day03-polygons-taco/)** — Carto to Taco: Real places shaped like taco ingredients (Wainwright shell, Chonos beef, Iceland lettuce, Zadar cheese, Iowa tomato, La Pampa onion, Halmahera cilantro, Montalbán sour cream)
